Kasama Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Kasama ("the app") is a shared-living app for boarding house, dorm and apartment roommates to track bills, chores and announcements together. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • A display name. Kasama does not use accounts, emails or passwords. Opening the app for the first time creates an anonymous session tied to your device, and you give it a display name.
  • A profile photo (optional). If you choose one, it's uploaded from your photo library.
  • Content you post. Bills, chores, announcements, and receipt photos you add to your household's board.
  • A push notification token, so your device can be notified about activity in your household (a new bill, a chore due, an announcement). You can decline this permission and still use the app.

What we don't collect

No email address, no phone number, no password, no location data, and no advertising or analytics identifiers. Kasama has no third-party analytics or ad SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Who can see it

Everything you post is visible only to the other members of your household — the people who joined using your household's invite code. Nothing is public.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider, in a Postgres database and file storage bucket. Access is restricted by row-level security so a household can only ever read its own data.

How long we keep it

Your content stays in your household until you delete it, your household deletes it, or you leave/sign out of the household (which hands off anything you owed and removes your membership). To request full deletion of your data, contact us at the address below.

Children

Kasama is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.
